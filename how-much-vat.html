Is your print 0% or 20% VAT?

How Much VAT? is a free UK print VAT checker. Type any print item, such as orders of service, flyers, menus or banners, and it tells you whether HMRC guidance treats it as 0% or 20% VAT, with the reason and any conditions.

It is a small, single-purpose tool, built in the same spirit as our Shopify apps: one problem, solved properly.
